How to use it

1. Add photos. Tap Add photos or any empty circle. On a phone this lets the customer take a new photo or pick one from their gallery.

2. Fit the picture. Drag inside the circle to move it, and use the zoom slider (or pinch / mouse scroll) so the important part sits inside the badge. Rotate if the photo is sideways.

3. Print. Pick your Badge size at the top, then click Print sheet to print every badge at true size, ready for the press.

The dashed circle is the safe area — keep faces and text inside it. Everything wraps at true size for a 300 DPI print. Your photos never leave this page.
