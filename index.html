Who we are

Meet the founders ✨

Arjun
Co-Founder · Age 8

Arjun is the creative director of Arni Startup! He loves designing bold, colourful badge pins and magnets. His favourite part of the job is coming up with brand-new designs and seeing people smile when they wear them.

Niya
Co-Founder · Age 7

Niya is the jewelry queen of Arni Startup! She handcrafts every bracelet and pair of earrings with care and patience. She believes every piece should make the person wearing it feel extra special and beautiful.
